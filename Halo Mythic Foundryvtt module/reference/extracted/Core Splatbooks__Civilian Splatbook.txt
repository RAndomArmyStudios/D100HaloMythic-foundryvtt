THE CIVILIAN SPLATBOOK 
The following is a small splatbook that allows GMs and Players to 
expand the options available to them at Character Creation. This 
Splatbook offers a handful of options that Players may choose for 
their Civilians at Character Creation that give them benefits that they 
may bring into future scenarios. 
 
CIVILIAN SPECIALIZATION PACKAGES 
This Splatbook adds the addition of new Civilian-only Specialization 
Packages that allow them to expand what the Characters are capable 
of after Character Creation. 
 This ignores the Civilian’s Specialization Limitation rule. At 
Creation, the players are able to choose a single Civilian Specialization 
package. 
 
SPECIALIZATION PACKAGE COSTS 
Some Civilian Specialization Packages will have a cost to select, this is 
to help balance them out compared to some of the other less robust 
options. 
 
PACKAGE CHARACTERISTIC ADVANCEMENTS 
Some Civilian Specialization Packages will list a Characteristic that it 
gives a +5 to. This is the Package giving the Character a Characteristic 
Advancement, and not increasing the overall Characteristic.  
 The Characteristic Advancements any of these Packages 
give will only cover the very first Characteristic Advancement of that 
Characteristic and will not cover any further advancements such as 
bringing the Characteristic to a +10. 
 
OTHER SOLDIER TYPES 
It is not advised to allow other Soldier Types to take from these 
Specialization Packages, as they are significantly weaker than the 
combat-based Specialization Packages that are available in the main 
Handbook. Though if the GM allows it, then Players may choose one 
of the Civilian Specialization Packages instead of one from the main 
handbook. 
 
COVENANT SPECIES 
The players and GM may work together to have the Civilian 
Specialization Packages make sense for their character and game. 
There is a chance not every Civilian Specialization Package could make 
sense for a Covenant character to choose from. This is something to 
consider when selecting Packages. 
 
BEGINNING WITH cR 
GMs should not limit Players’ beginning cR amounts based on their 
jobs. cR is a major balancing aspect of the game and allowing Players 
to have more than others can create massive imbalance within the 
party. 
 
BECOMING A SOLDIER 
When becoming a Soldier using the rules in the main Mythic 
Handbook, the Players may still choose a standard Specialization Pack 
from the main Mythic Handbook. Though the GM should enforce a 
large time  skip so the Characters have time in -universe to gather 
these new Skills and Abilities. 4 to 6 months should do well for this.

SOLDIER TYPE SPECIAL RULES 
Purchasing anything from Covenant or Forerunner Armories are 
impossible. UNSC Weapons are at triple the price, while weapons 
with the Insurrectionist Tag [I] are at double the price. 
The Civilian may begin with up to 2 Educations at half their 
Experience Cost. 
SQUAD UP:  When with other Civilians, the Civilian gains +5 
Courage.  
SPECIALIZATION LIMITATIONS 
The Civilian may only choose from the Civilian Specialization 
Packages.

and +10 becomes +20. If the Character already has an Ability given by the Specialization Pack, the Play er or GM may decide one of equal or lesser 
Experience Cost to replace it.
