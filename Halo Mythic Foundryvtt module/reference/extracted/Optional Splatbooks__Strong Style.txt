USING STRONG STYLE 
Strong Style is a Pro Wrestling and Martial Arts expansion. Strong 
Style is designed to be used alongside any 100DOS game or expansion. 
 
WRESTLING MOVE CATEGORIES 
In Strong Style, there are three main categories of Wrestling Moves 
that you will find: Holds, Throws, and Attacks.  Each Wrestling Move 
category will have several sub -types based on how those moves are 
performed, as well as possible connecting rules and interactions. 
 
HOLDS 
A Hold is a technical maneuver used to control, immobilize, or 
damage an Opponent to gain a strategic advantage in combat. These 
are techniques that utilize leverage, body weight, and strength in 
order to manipulate joints and to target specific muscles . Holds are 
moves that can be held indefinitely, dealing their Damage and listed 
effects with each Turn the User is still able to keep control. 
 It’s important to know that Holds will generally target the 
Joint of the limb they are targeting. Most will target the shoulder, 
neck, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, and ankle.

THROWS 
A Throw is any high-impact maneuver that is used by lifting, throwing, 
or otherwise diverting an Opponent to the ground.  These are moves 
that are performed specifically to deal damage and gain control of 
combat, especially when attempting to keep an Opponent both Prone 
or in compromised positions for further Attacks to be made.

ATTACKS 
Attacks can be a confusing term, as it’s a term often used in Mythic, 
so in these scenarios, we’ll call these Wrestling Attacks. Wrestling 
Attacks are the official term used for any impact and strike -based 
move where the User’s own body is the weapon. 
 Wrestling Attacks are offensive maneuvers, primarily 
strikes, used to weaken and quickly Damage an Opponent. Unlike 
Holds and Throws, Attacks are Melee Attacks, such as punches, 
elbows, kicks, and knee strikes. Wrestling Attacks offer an expansion 
of Mythic’s Unarmed Melee Attacks system.

WRESTLING TRAINING 
Each Wrestling Move Category must first be learned before it can be 
utilized correctly. All Wrestling Moves use the “Weapon Training” 
system. This means that if you do not have the needed Wrestling 
Training, you will have a -20 Penalty to perform any move. Opposing 
a Wrestling Move does not need the listed Training.

WRESTLING SPECIALIZATION 
Wrestling Specialization is a system that allows a Character to 
improve their ability to use Sub -Categories of Wrestling Moves.  In 
order to begin taking Wrestling Specializations, the Character must 
first have the Wrestling Training that Subcategory belongs in. 
 Wrestling Specializations give a +5 Bonus to performing any 
Wrestling Move in that Subcategory.  Each Subcategory can have up 
to 3 Specializations taken. The previous Specialization must always be 
purchased before the next can be taken.

SIGNATURE MOVES 
A Signature Move is a Wrestling Move that has bene honed with both 
expertise and knowledge.  A Character can have  up to  3 Signature 
Moves. A Signature Move can only be chosen from a Subcategory that 
has Intermediate Specialization. 
 When the Character chooses a Signature Move, they will 
select a Buff and pay that Buff’s Experience Cost.  A Signature Move 
can only have a single Buff applied to it.  
 A Character’s Signature Move can be changed by spending 
50 Experience. This can only be performed between missions.

BUILT TOUGH TRAIT 
The Built Tough Trait shows up often in 100DOS expansions. It gives 
the Character Bonuses against Grapple Tests or anything that would 
knock them over. It will also give them this Bonus in Opposed Tests 
when fighting against Wrestling Throws or Holds that would lift, move, 
or knock them over. 
 
DEGREES OF SUCCESS 
Degrees of Success are more important in this expansion than in any 
other 100DOS content released thus far. It is important to understand 
the terminology that you will see commonly in this expansion. 
 If an Opposed Test is used, and it calls for a Character to 
check if they have (X) Degrees of Success to gain an effect, this means 
that only that Character’s Degrees of Success in the Opposed Test will 
matter. No amount of Degrees of Success of their Opponent will 
matter unless the Opponent won the Opposed Test overall. 
 If an Opposed Test is used and it calls for a Character to 
check if they have (X) Degrees of Success over the User, this means 
that they must have that many more Degrees of Success compared to 
their Opponent. For example, if a move would gain additional Damage 
if the User had 3 or more Degrees of Success compared to the 
Opponent in the Opposed Test, this means that they must have 3 
more than their Opponent. This also accounts for Degrees of Failure, 
as well. If the User had 1 Degree of Success while the Op ponent had 
4 Degrees of Failure, the User will have enough Degrees of Success 
over the Opponent to gain the additional Damage.

HOLD, SLAM, AND BREAK DAMAGE 
Slam and Break Damage are similar to Punch and Kick Damage found 
in the main handbook. Depending on the type of move being used, it 
may call for Slam or Break Damage. These types of Damage are 
dependent on the User’s Size Category. The larger that Character is, 
the more possible Damage their move will deal. 
 Hold will always do a minimum of 1 Damage from the 
Damage rolled, even if Mini or Small would have reduced it to 0. Not 
all Holds will deal Damage. It is impor tant to make sure you check 
each Hold and how they interact with the Opponent.

MOVES AND SPECIAL DAMAGE 
All moves that roll a D10 will be able to deal Special Damage, 
regardless of how the move is performed. 
 Wrestling Holds have a unique mechanic for when they deal 
Special Damage. Whenever a Hold deals Special Damage, the Special 
Damage will be the Damage the Hold would deal, plus  half of  the 
User’s Characteristic that was used to perform the Opposed Test. 
 Each Mythic Characteristic the User has of a matching 
Characteristic will add +2 to the Special Damage. 
 For example, if the Opponent was using their Warfare 
Melee Characteristic of 52 in the Opposed Test, they will add 26 
Special Damage to the roll. 
 The Special Damage will always strike the location of the 
body that the Move is being performed on or the Body Location that 
was impacted. 
 
MYTHIC CHARACTERISTIC DAMAGE 
Any source of Damage that would list adding a Characteristic will also 
add that Character’s Mythic Characteristic as well.  This goes for all 
Characteristics in the system. 
 For example, if a move called for adding the User’s Strength 
Modifier, it will also add the User’s Mythic Strength Modifier. 
 If a move calls for half of the User’s Strength Modifier, only 
half of the User’s Mythic Strength will be added, rounding down, to a 
minimum of 1.  
 
MOVES AND DAMAGE RESISTANCE 
Unless specified otherwise, all of each type of move will interact with 
a Character’s Damage Resistance in the same way. 
• All Holds will ignore Damage Resistance. 
• All Throws will ignore Armor. 
• All Attacks will interact with Damage Resistance normally. 
 
DISLOCATED LIMBS 
Some moves are able to dislocate limbs that they are being performed 
on. If this happens, the Character will be at the listed Penalty until 
they receive a Full Action Medication Test. This Medication Test will 
relocate the limb back into its socket, reducing the Penalties to -10. 
 The Dislocated Limb ’s Penalty can be completely fixed by 
receiving an Extended Medication Test. 
 
BREAKING OUT OF MOVES 
When a Wrestling Move lists how many Half or Full Actions it takes 
for when an Opponent breaks out of the move, that is not the amount 
of the Opponent’s Actions it takes, that’s how much of the User of the 
move’s Actions it takes up for their current Turn. If it lists a Half Action, 
that means that the User will only have a Half Action left of their Turn. 
If it lists a Full Action, the User’s Turn will be used up attempting and 
failing to perform the move. 
 If a move does not list what Action  is lost when an 
Opponent breaks out of a move, it will take the Action listed to 
perform the move. If a move takes 2 Full Actions, it will instead take 
a Full Action from the User when escaped. 
 
MOVES THAT LEAVE THE USER PRONE 
There are Wrestling Moves that leave the User Prone after they are 
performed. When this happens, the User can make a +10 Agility Test 
at the end of their Turn. If successful, they stand  before their Turn 
ends. This may be Opposed by the Opponent that was also left Prone, 
where the Opponent will have a -20 Penalty on the Test to see who 
stands first. 
 If the Opponent had 3 or less Degrees of Success over the 
User in the Opposed Test, they would stand first, leaving the User in 
a Crouching position as the Opponent begins their next turn. 
 If the User Opponent had 3 or more Degrees of Success over 
the User in the Opposed Test, they will stand first, leaving the User in 
a Prone position as the Opponent begins their next Turn. 
 If the User is successful, they will be considered standing 
during the Opponent’s next Turn. 
 
USING WRESTLING MOVES IN GRAPPLES 
A Wrestling Move can be performed as part of a Grapple, allowing the 
Character to use their Turn to transition into a Hold or a Throw. When 
this happens, a Controlling Character would gain  a +10 Bonus to all 
Wrestling Moves used, as well as any benefits that they would receive 
from a Grapp le, such as  Mythic Characteristic used from Opposed 
Tests that a Grapple would give.  This would also give the benefits of 
Traits and Abilities that would benefit Grapples.

HEAD AND SHOULDER HOLDS 
 
CAMEL CLUTCH 
Action: Full Action Perform: Controller 
Prerequisite: Opponent must be Prone 
The User sits on their Opponent’s back and places both of the 
Opponent’s arms around their thighs, then reaches around the 
Opponent’s head and applies a chinlock -style chokehold. The User 
then pulls back, applying pressure to the throat and spine. 
 For every Round the Opponent is in the Camel Clutch, they 
will gain take Hold + (X) Damage, ignoring Damage Resistance . (X) is 
equal to half of the Character’s Strength Modifier. 
To attempt to break out, the Opponent must make a  -20 
Penalty Opposed Strength Test against the User, where the User is 
able to use either their Warfare Melee or Strength Characteristic. If 
successful, the Opponent breaks free. If failed, they are still trapped 
in the hold. If failed by 3 or more Degrees of Failure,  the Character 
will take +1 level of Fatigue. 
 
CHINLOCK 
Action: Full Action Perform: Controller 
Prerequisite: Prone Opponent or facing away from User 
The User grabs the Opponent’s head and neck  from behind with the 
sole purpose of gripping the Opponent’s chin to wrench it to the side. 
This hold is less about choking the Opponent and more about 
damaging their neck tendons. 
 When performed while standing and the Opponent is facing 
away, it is a Chinlock. when performed while standing and facing the 
Opponent, it is a Reverse Chinlock. When performed on a Prone 
Opponent facing the ground, it is an STS  or Crossface. When 
performed on a Prone Opponent who is facing upward, it is an 
Inverted Cravate. 
 For every Round the Opponent is in the Chinlock, they must 
make an Opposed Test against the User. The Opponent can use their 
Strength, Agility, or Warfare Melee Characteristic. The User can use 
their Strength or Warfare Melee Characteristic. 
 If successful, the Opponent will slip out of the hold. If failed, 
the Opponent will take Damage.  When the User is Damaging the 
Opponent, it will deal Hold + (X) Damage, ignoring Armor. (X) is equal 
to the User’s Strength Modifier and Warfare Melee Modifier, plus the 
amount of Degrees of Failure the Opponent made in their Test. 
 
FACELOCK 
Action: Full Action Perform: Controller 
Prerequisite: Opponent must be Standing 
The User tucks the Opponent’s head into the User’s armpit and wraps 
his arm around the head so that the forearm is pressed against the 
face. The User then grabs their own arm with their free hand to lock 
in the hold and compress the Opponent’s face. 
 For every Round the Opponent is in the Facelock, they must 
make an Opposed Test. Both the User and the Opponent will use 
either their Strength or Warfare Melee Characteristic. If successful, 
the Opponent will escape. If failed, the Opponent will take +1 level of 
Fatigue for every 2 difference between the User’s Degrees of Success 
and the Opponent’s Degrees of Failure. 
 The Facelock’s true utility is the follow-up that the User can 
perform once they’ve succeeded in at least one Opposed Test. The 
User is able to perform a Drop or Slam as a Half Action. The Opponent 
will gain a -20 Penalty to Opposing any of these follow-up Actions that 
are taken. 
 General follow -ups include Elbow Drops, DDTs, 
Neckbreaker Slams, Backbreakers, Forearm Smash, etc. Generally any 
move that has the User behind the Opponent to perform. 
HEADSCISSORS (Grounded) 
Action: Full Action Perform: Controller 
Prerequisite: Crouching or Prone Opponent. 
Headscissors have two variants, a grounded Headscissors, and a 
Flying Headscissors. We’ll first start with the Headscissors. 
 The grounded variant of the Headscissors has the User 
approach a Prone or Crouching Opponent. The User will approach the 
Opponent and placing their legs around the User’s head and neck, 
crossing the top leg after it’s gone around the Opponent’s chin. The 
User then tightens the grip to choke the Opponent via compression. 
 For every Round the Opponent is in the Headscissors, they 
must make an Opposed Strength or Warfare Melee Characteristic 
Test to attempt to break out. The User will gain a +10 Bonus to this 
Test when using Strength to Oppose the Test.  If the Opponent is 
successful, they will escape the Hold. If failed, the User will gain +1 
level of Fatigue for every Degrees of Success that the User has on their 
Opposed Test, to a maximum of their Strength Modifier in Fatigue 
gained. 
 
HEADSCISSORS (Flying) 
Action: Full Action Perform: Controller 
Prerequisite: Standing or Crouching Opponent. 
Flying Headscissors sees the User jumping into the air to attempt to 
wrap and cross their legs around the Opponent’s head and neck while 
standing or crouching.  The User will then have multiple follow -up 
options depending on if this was successful. 
 To perform this Hold, the User will jump and make a -10 
Penalty Stunting Test, Opposing the Opponent’s Agility or Warfare 
Melee Characteristic. If successful, they can perform the Headscissors 
Hold where the User will continue to constrict their legs around the 
Opponent’s neck and attempt to cause them to drop prone. For every 
Round the Opponent is in this hold, they must make a -10 Strength 
Test. If failed while standing, they will drop to a Crouching position. If 
failed with 3 or more Degrees of Failure,  they will drop to a Prone 
position. If the Opponent drops to a Prone position, the User can 
transition to another similar hold, such as the Headscissors (Ground) 
Hold as a Free Action. 
 If the User had 2 or more Degrees of Success on the initial 
Stunting Test to perform this move, they can instantly transition this 
move to the Headscissors Takedown. 
 
NELSON 
Action: Full Action Perform: Controller 
Prerequisite: Opponent’s back must be towards the User 
The User stands behind the Opponent and wraps one or both of the 
User’s arms und erneath the Opponent’s armpits and locks their 
hands behind the Opponent’s neck. The User pushes the back of the 
Opponent’s head forward and down against their chest. 
 For every Round the Opponent is in the Nelson, they must 
make a -10 Penalty Opposed Strength or Warfare Melee Test against 
the User . If the Opponent succeeds, they will escape. If failed, the 
Opponent will take +1 level of Fatigue. If the Opponent fails with 3 or 
more Degrees of Failure, the User will add half of their Strength 
Modifier to additional levels of Fatigue against the Opponent. 
 If the Opponent is successful with 2 or more Degrees of 
Success over the Opponent, they can transition the Nelson into a 
Suplex or Slam of choice where the Opponent faces away from the 
User.

STUMP PULLER 
Action: Full Action Perform: Controller 
Prerequisite: Opponent’s back must be on ground, Prone 
The Opponent is Crouching or Prone on the ground, while the User 
stands behind them. The User straddles the next with their legs , 
reaches down, and grabs the Opponent’s leg, and pulls up.  This 
stretches the Opponent’s hamstring while compressing their neck, 
giving ample torque against both segments of the body. 
 For every Round the Opponent is in the Stump Puller, they 
must make an Opposed Strength Test against the User’s Strength or 
Warfare Melee Characteristic. If successful, the Opponent rolls free. 
If failed, the Opponent will take Hold + (X) Damage, where (X) is half 
of the User’s Strength Modifier plus half of their Warfare Melee 
Modifier added together. If the Opponent fails the Test with 3 or more 
Degrees of Failure, they will gain +1 level of Fatigue. 
 If the User gains 4 or more Degrees of Success over the 
Opponent, they can move the User from Crouch to Prone as a Free 
Action, or from Prone to Crouch.  Every Turn the Opponent is in this 
Hold, the Degrees of Success needed is reduced by 1. 
 
WING TEARER 
Action: Full Action Perform: Controller 
Prerequisite: Opponent’s back must be on ground, Prone 
The Opponent must be face -down on the ground, Prone, or facing 
away while Crouching. The User will grab the Opponent’s head and 
brace against the Opponent’s spine with their shin. The User reaches 
down and grabs both of the Opponent’s wrists and begins pulling up 
to hyperextend both of their shoulders. 
 For every Round the Opponent is in the Wing Tearer, they 
must make an Opposed Strength Test against the User’s Strength 
Characteristic. If successful, the Opponent will break free and roll out. 
If the Opponent fails, the User will hyperextend their should ers, 
dealing Hold + (X) Damage. (X) is equal to the User’s Strength Modifier, 
plus 1 for every Degree of Success made on the Test. If the User has 4 
or more Degrees of Success over the Opponent, the Opponent’s 
shoulder (roll randomly) will be dislocated. If successful with 6 or 
more Degrees of Success, both shoulders will be dislocated. 
 Dislocated shoulders will be at a -30 Penalty on Tests using 
that Arm in any Action. 
 
ARM HOLDS 
 
ARMBAR 
Action: Full Action Perform: Controller 
Prerequisite: None 
The User will grab the Opponent’s arm, twist it, and put pressure on 
their shoulder and elbow. The User will take the Opponent’s arm and 
press it into the Opponent’s back while moving behind them. 
 For every Round the Opponent is in an Armbar, the 
Opponent must make an Opposed Strength Test against the User’s 
Strength or Warfare Melee Characteristic.  If the Opponent is 
successful, they will take no Damage. If the Opponent is successful 
with 3 or more Degrees of Success over the User, they will escape. 
 If the User is successful, the Opponent will take  Hold + (X) 
Damage, where (X) is equal to half of the User’s Strength Modifier , 
ignoring Damage Resistance. For every Turn the Opponent is in this,

Action: Full Action Perform: Controller

The User sits next to the Opponent’s head, facing them. The User’s

take no Fatigue and will continue to be able to breathe. 
 If the User is successful, the Opponent will be unable to

of Success over the Opponent, the Opponent will gain + 2 levels of 
Fatigue. 
 Once an Opponent has gone Unconscious from Fatigue 
when this move is performed against them, they will begin 
Suffocating.

 If the User is successful, the Opponent will take  Hold + (X)

 If the User is successful, the Opponent will take  Hold + (X)

AIRPLANE SPIN 
Action: Full Action Perform: Any 
Prerequisite: N/A 
The User lifts the Opponent onto their shoulders and begins to spin in 
a circle in an attempt to make them dizzier than you will be. The User 
can spin as long as they can possibly attempt to. 
 A version of this where the User grabs the Opponent by the 
legs and spins them in a circle is known as a Giant Swing. 
 During an Airplane Spin, both the User and the Opponent 
must make separate, non -Opposed Toughness Tests. For every 2 
Degrees of Failure on these Tests, the Character will gain +1 level of 
Fatigue. Each consecutive Turn, the User will gain a stacking -5 Penalty 
for this Toughness Test, while the Opponent will gain a stacking -10 
Penalty. These Penalties max out at -40. 
 The Opponent may attempt to break free with a -20 Penalty 
Opposed Test using either Strength, Agility, or Warfare Melee to 
escape. If the Opponent is successful, they will escape as a Half Action 
as if the move was a Hold. 
 At the end of the User’s Turn, they are able to end the 
Airplane Spin as a Free Action. This is done by allowing the Opponent 
to fall to the ground.  If the User is doing a Giant Swing, they will leg 
go and the Opponent will Scatter (X) meters, to a minimum of 0. (X) is 
equal to the Character’s Strength or Agility Modifier, whatever is 
highest. The distance is then reduced by 1 for every 50 kg the 
Opponent weighs, to a minimum of 0. 
 
ARM DRAG 
Action: Half Action Perform: Any 
Prerequisite: Both Characters must be standing. 
The User grabs the Opponent’s arm and uses their momentum 
against them. The User hooks the Opponent’s arm and flips them over 
their own body, dropping them Prone on the ground.  The User may 
also roll as they perform this move.  There are many variants of the 
Arm Drag, but all of them will use this entry. 
 This is performed if the Opponent has been making a 
Movement Action towards the User, or if the User is close enough to 
be grabbed and tossed. If the Opponent is not moving, the Arm Drag 
can still be performed, but the User must roll while performing the 
move against them. 
 The User and the Opponent will roll anywhere from 1 meter 
to half of the User’s Half Move in meters. It is up to the User how far 
they will attempt to move. 
 The Opponent must make an Opposed Agility or Warfare 
Melee Test against the User. If successful, they will be able to roll out 
of the Arm Drag without taking Damage. 
 If the User is successful , the Opponent will be slammed 
onto the ground, taking 1D5+(X) Damage, ignoring Armor. (X) is equal 
to the User’s Strength Modifier or Warfare Melee Modifier, 
whichever is highest, plus the amount of meters the Opponent moved 
in the Turn. If the Opponent did not move prior, the amount of meters 
the Opponent moved will be replaced by the amount of meters the 
User rolled while performing the move. If the User decided to roll 
while the Opponent was also moving, the highest of the two are taken. 
 The Arm Drag can be used alongside a Delayed Action to 
catch a moving Opponent off guard as they approach into the User’s 
Melee Reach. If a Delayed Action is used this way alongside the Arm 
Drag, the User will gain a +20 Bonus to the Opposed Test. 
 The Arm Drag can be used alongside a Delayed Action to 
interrupt an Opponent’s Hold or move that involves grabbing the 
User’s arm, as well. This will initiate the usual Opposed Test, but 
instead of breaking free if the User is successful, they will perform the 
Arm Drag on the Opponent. 
 
BACK BODY DROP 
Action: Half or Full Action Perform: Any 
Prerequisite: Must be able to Lift the Opponent. 
The Back Body Drop has the User bend forward or Crouch in front of 
their Opponent , grab them, and lift them overhead, attempting to 
either throw them, or use the Opponent’s momentum against them. 
The User will do this in order to hurl the Opponent over their back so 
they have a rough landing. 
 The Back Body Drop will take a Full Action for the User to 
attempt to perform the Back Body Drop against them. However, if the 
Opponent is making a Movement Action towards the User, the User 
will be able to quickly use this momentum against them, performing 
the Back Body Drop as a Half Action, instead. This makes the Back 
Body Drop a good move when combining it with a Delayed Action to 
catch an incoming Opponent off-guard. 
 When performing the Back Body Drop, the Opponent must 
make an Opposed Warfare Melee or Agility Test against the User’s 
Strength or Warfare Melee Characteristic to attempt to escape. 
 If the Opponent is successful with 3 or more Degrees of 
Success over the User in the Opposed Test, they will quickly escape 
from being thrown by this move.  
 If the Opponent is successful but with less than 3 Degrees 
of Success, they will still be thrown but will take no Damage or ill 
effects as they successfully slide off of the User’s back or land with 
ease. The Opponent may decide whether they are thrown without 
taking Damage, or if they will safely slide directly behind the User.  
 If the User is successful, the Opponent will be thrown (X) 
meters behind them, where (X) is 1 for every 3 Strength Modifier of 
the User, to a minimum of 1, plus the  one-third of the  Opponent’s 
movement speed as they were caught by the move (to a minimum of 
0, rounding down).  
 When the Opponent lands on the ground,  they will take 
Slam + (X) Damage, where (X) is the same as the meters they were 
thrown, multiplied by 3. This Damage ignores Armor. For example, if 
a Normal Sized Character throws an Opponent 4 meters, they will 
take 2D10 + 12 Damage. Depending on how far  the Opponent ends 
up falling, the GM may add Fall Damage to the amount of Damage 
being taken. 
 
CRUCIFIX TOSS 
Action: Half or Full Action Perform: Any 
Prerequisite: Both Characters must be standing or crouching. 
The User lifts the Opponent above their back with the Opponent’s

average, it is only a Half Action to perform this move. If the Opponent

 The Opponent must make an Opposed Warfare Melee or

 If the User is successful, they will throw down the Opponent

Damage from the Opponent’s weight. 
 If the User had 3 or more Degrees of Success over the 
Opponent on the Opposed Test, the Opponent will land on their neck 
and head, increasing the Damage by +1D10.

 The Opponent must make a -10 Penalty Opposed Test using

 If the User is successful with 4 or more Degrees of Success

into an Armbar, Headscissors (Grounded), Facelock, Short Arm

BULLDOG

Prerequisite: Both Characters must be standing. 
The Bulldog is a move where the User grabs an Opponent’s head and 
jumps forward and lands in a sitting or Prone position. This will drive 
the Opponent’s head into the ground. There are various variants of 
the Bulldog, each with a different Action to perform. There are many 
types of Bulldogs and names of them, as well. This includes Cutters, 
the most famous of the Cutters being the Diamond Cutter and the 
RKO. These will all be performed the same, regardless of name. 
 
STANDARD BULLDOG: INVERTED, REVERSE, ONE-HANDED, ETC. 
These Bulldogs are all a Half Action to perform and will deal the same 
type of Damage and effect regardless of the choice.  These are 
performed from a standing position where both Opponents are 
within Melee Reach. 
 When used, the Opponent must make an Opposed Agility 
or Warfare Melee Test. If the Opponent is successful, they will slip 
free from the Bulldog before they’re driven to the ground. 
 If the Opponent is successful with 3 or more Degrees of 
Success over the User, the Opponent will stay standing and the User 
will be left Prone on the ground from failing the Bulldog. 
 If the User is successful, the Opponent will be left Prone on 
the ground and will take Breaker + (X) Damage. (X) is equal to half of 
the User’s Warfare Melee Modifier and Agility Modifier added 
together, rounding down to a minimum of 2. This Damage ignores 
Armor. 
 If the User is successful with 4 or more Degrees of Success 
over the Opponent in the Opposed Test, they can transition the 
Bulldog into a Head, Neck, or Shoulder Hold of choice that does not 
involve choking the Opponent with the legs. 
 
SPINNING BULLDOG 
The Spinning Bulldog is a Full Action to perform, as the User will grab 
the Opponent diagonally, spinning them and dropping them back-first 
on the ground with additional momentum from the rotation. 
 When performed, it will follow the same rules as the 
standard Bulldog but the Opponent will gain a -10 Penalty to Oppose 
the Spinning Bulldog, and the full Agility Modifier will be added to the 
Damage instead of Half Agility Modifier. 
 
RUNNING BULLDOG 
The Running Bulldog can only be performed at the end of a User’s Full 
Action Move, Charge Move, or Run Move Action . This is a technical 
Full Action to perform due to it being paired with a Movement Action. 
 When performed, it will follow the same rules as the 
standard Bulldog but the Damage will be increased. The Damage from 
the move will gain a Bonus equal to the amount of meters the User 
moved in the Turn it was used, as well as giving the User’s full Agility 
Modifier when figuring the Damage, instead of Half Agility Modifier. 
 
 
 
BACKBREAKER and GUTBUSTER

The Backbreaker is a common move where the User lifts the 
Opponent into the air and does one of a few variants of dropping 
them onto the User’s legs , impacting onto their back.  Depending on 
the version used, the Characters will take various amounts of Damage 
and be left in various positions after the move is performed.  There 
are many types of Backbreakers, though most will follow one of the 
given sets of rules, regardless of name. 
 The Gutbuster is when the User drops the Opponent 
stomach or chest-first onto their knee, instead of dropping them onto 
their back. Regardless of this difference, the Damage is the same. 
 
STANDARD BACKBREAKER: FRONT, INVERSE, DROP, MAT, ETC. 
These Backbreakers are a Full Action to perform and will deal the 
same type of Damage and effect regardless of the choice. These are

Opponent to drop them onto their knee. This move will leave the User 
in a crouching position after it is performed. 
 When used, the Opponent must make an Opposed Agility 
or Warfare Melee Test. If the Opponent is successful, they will slip 
free from the Backbreaker before it’s performed. 
 If the User is successful, the Opponent will be left Prone on

knee. The Opponent will take Breaker + (X) Damage. (X) is equal to 
half of the User’s Strength Modifier and Warfare Melee Modi fier 
added together, rounding down to a minimum of 1.  This Damage 
ignores Armor. 
 If the User is successful with 2 or more Degrees of Success 
over the Opponent in the Opposed Test, the User is able to position 
the Opponent in the desired position after the move is performed. 
This includes the direction the Opponent is facing, whether they’re 
left on the User’s knee, or if they’re left Crouching or Prone. 
 If the User is successful with 4 or more Degrees of Success 
over the Opponent in the Opposed Test, the User is able to transition 
to a Backbreaker Submission Hold. 
 
FALLING BACKBREAKER 
The Falling Backbreaker has the User dropping to their back as the

Opponent will then fall Prone onto the ground and the User will be 
left Prone as well.  With enough Degrees of Success, the User will be

are the Fireman’s Carry Backbreaker and the Falling Backbreaker. 
 When performed, the Falling Backbreaker will follow the

 If the User is successful with 5 or more Degrees of Success 
over the Opponent in the Opposed Test, the User is able to transition 
to a Backbreaker Submission Hold or to end the Turn standing.

DDT 
Action: Half or Full Action Perform: Any 
Prerequisite: Both Characters must be standing or crouching. 
The DDT is any move in which the User falls down or backwards in 
order to drive the Opponent’s head into the ground. Most DDTs 
involve quickly grabbing the Opponent by the head or by using a front 
facelock and  then falling backwards or forwards so that the 
Opponent’s head will collide with the ground. 
 Both the User and the Opponent must be within Point Blank 
range to each other and within Melee Reach. The User and Opponent 
must both either be Standing, or both must be Crouching for this 
move to properly be executed. 
 
STANDARD DDT:  
These DDTs are all a Half Action to perform and will deal the same 
type of Damage and effect regardless of the choice. These are 
performed from a standing position where both Opponents are 
within Melee Reach.  The Standard DDT covers Flip, Elevated, Float -
Over, Underhook, Cradle, Spike, and more. 
 When used, the Opponent must make an Opposed Agility 
or Warfare Melee Test. If the Opponent is successful  with 2 or more 
Degrees of Success, they will slip free from the Bulldog before they’re 
driven to the ground. 
 If the Opponent is successful with less than 2 Degrees of 
Success, they will still be taken to the Ground with the User, but will 
be able to stop the move before taking the Damage. 
 If the Opponent is successful with 3 or more Degrees of 
Success over the User  in the Opposed Test , the Opponent will stay 
standing and the User will be left Prone on the ground from being 
tripped while attempting the DDT. The Opponent may decide not to 
perform this additional action. 
 If the User is successful, the Opponent will be left Prone on 
the ground and will take Breaker + (X) Damage. (X) is equal to half of 
the User’s Strength Modifier and Warfare Melee  Modifier added 
together, rounding down to a minimum of 1. This Damage ignores 
Armor. 
 If the User is successful with 5 or more Degrees of Success 
over the Opponent in the Opposed Test, they can transition the DDT 
into a Head, Neck, or Shoulder Hold of choice that does not involve 
choking the Opponent with the legs.  This transition will reduce the 
move being performed from a Full Action to a Half Action. A Half 
Action can be performed as a Free Action, instead. 
 
TORNADO, SNAP, SPINNING, AND SWINGING DDT 
The Tornado, Spinning, Snap, and Swinging DDTs, as well as all of their 
variants, involve horizontal movement, pendulum -like swinging

perform compared to the Standard DDT’s Half Action. 
 When performed, it will follow the same rules as the 
standard DDT but the Opponent will gain a -10 Penalty to Oppose the 
DDT, and the full Agility Modifier will be added to the Damage instead 
of Half Agility Modifier. 
 
RUNNING DDT 
The Running DDT can only be performed at the end of a User’s Full 
Action Move, Charge Move, or Run Move Action. This is a technical 
Full Action to perform due to it being paired with a Movement Action. 
 When performed, it will follow the same rules as the 
standard DDT but the Damage will be increased. The Damage from 
the move will gain a Bonus equal to the amount of meters the User
