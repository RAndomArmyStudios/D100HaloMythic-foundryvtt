FORTIFICATIONS 
Fortifications is a splatbook designed to allow Players and the GM to 
build large military bases  by stringing together military buildings . 
These bases begin as a Firebase, which is a mobile military Base of 
Operations. There are two types of each building, a Modular 
Fabrication or a Designed Fortification. 
 
“PREFABRICATED” MODULAR POD FORTIFICATION 
Modular Pod Fortifications  are any Fortification that is mass -
produced based on modular utilities. These buildings are your default 
Fortifications and are capable of being implemented incredibly fast. 
Modular Pod Fortifications  are less resilient than Designed 
Fortifications, but trade defensive options for speed of construction. 
 When a Modular Pod Fortification is called in, Support 
Points and cR will be spent, and a Vehicle will use Aerial Vehicle 
Transports to drop the Pod Fortification in. 
 Once the Vehicle drops off the Fortification, it will take 5 
minutes (30 Turns) for the building to install itself  and be ready for 
use. 
 
DESIGNED FORTIFICATION 
Designed Fortifications are bases that are designed and built based 
on the location and needs of the building. These buildings take longer 
to design and construct but offer much more powerful defenses and 
abilities. These also allow the owner to have much more control over 
the buildings and their layout. 
 Designed Fortifications will have +50% of the Breakpoints 
and +25% of the Armor of the listed Pod Fortifications. 
 Designed Fortifications do not cost Support Points to build. 
Instead, these buildings only require cR and time for construction. 
 
UNIVERSAL FORTIFICATIONS 
A Universal Fortification is a Fortification that all factions can use, 
each having their own variant. Each variant will have the same 
Characteristics and Abilities. 
At the top of each of these Universal Fortifications, the top 
green row is the UNSC name, the next purple row is the Covenant 
name, and the final red row is the Banished name. 
 
BUILDING CONSTRUCTION 
For the UNSC, construction of buildings is designed to be fast, 
standardized, and modular. Prefabricated Construction is common in 
the world of Halo, where standardized components are assembled 
and disassembled easily. This allowed for rapid deployment on newly 
established Designed fortifications. 
 Construction of a Building will take 1 day for every 750 
Square Meters. If a Character wants the building to be less uniform 
and standardized, they can have an architect design a building for 
them. Non-standardized buildings will instead take 1 day for every 
450 Square Meters. 
 If a building is being purchased in an area that needs to first 
be cleared out, such as rubble, trees, or on very uneven land, it will 
take 14 days to finalize the land for construction. 
 
UNIQUE BUILDINGS 
Unique Buildings are Buildings that only one or two factions have 
access to, instead of everyone.  The GM may allow factions to have 
access to these Unique Buildings at a price increase of 2x. 
 
FORTIFICATION UPGRADES 
Fortification Upgrades allow a Character to invest and improve their 
buildings and bases by improving what the building is capable of, 
whether this be through their Armor and Structure, or by improving 
their Capabilities. 
 
FORTIFICATION COVER 
Fortification Cover allows a Character to call in various forms of cover 
and protective utilities for their bases or general use on the 
battlefield. Fortification Cover is unique compared to the main 
handbook’s use of Cover. These will offer the size of the object, as 
well as the Armor and Structure of each. Think of them are more in -
depth compared to standard Cover, where the Armor is basically the 
Cover Points . Like Fortifications, Fortification Cover will also have 
Capabilities.

CALLING IN BUILDINGS 
Calling in Buildings uses a specialized  Hauler Dropship, using either 
the UNSC Heron , the Covena nt’s Iqnucekk -pattern variant of the 
Maravelotz-pattern Civilian Transport, and the Banished Siege Hauler. 
 The following table will list the Support Point information 
when calling in a building using the Hauler Dropship option. 
 This can only be used to drop in Modular Pod Fortifications. 
However, they may also be used to drop in the materials needed to 
build the Designed Fortifications . They cannot bring in pre -built 
Designed Fortifications. 
 Hauler Dropships cost no cR or Support Points to call in if a 
Building was brought in using it. However, if one is used for other 
means, the Hauler Dropship will cost +10 cR. 
 
TRANSPORT EXAMPLE 
Hauler 
Dropship 
An unarmed Dropship carries in the Pod 
Fortification and drops it at the desired location 
with pinpoint accuracy. The Hauler Dropship takes 
3 Minutes (30 Rounds) to arrive and will take 5 Full 
Actions to align and safely drop the building.

BUILDING CAPABILITY UPGRADE 
COST 
400 cR per Building 
CHARACTERISTICS 
SIZE SUPPORT POINTS 
N/A 1 
CAPABILITY 
The Building Capability Upgrade is an upgrade that the Player decides what 
Capability is upgraded. This Upgrade will increase the capability of one specified 
aspect or variable by 50%. 
 For example, The UNSC Reactor generates 500 Power and sends its 
Power up to 100 Meters from the Building. The Character may decide to increase 
the 500 Power to 750 Power, or they may decide to increase the distance the 
building is able to power to 150 Meters. 
 This may only be applied to a variable; however this could be applied 
to the same building a second time to affect a different variable. For example, the 
Player could increase both the Power and distance of the Reactor with two 
separate upgrades. 
 The GM may always decide whether a variable is far too powerful to 
give a +50% to.
