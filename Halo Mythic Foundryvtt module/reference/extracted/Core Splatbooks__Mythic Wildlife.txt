MYTHIC 
WILDLIFE 
 
A Wildlife Bestiary expansion for Mythic 6.3 and newer. 
 
 
 
 
 
Mythic Wildlife is a splatbook that introduces some wildlife entries found in the Halo franchise. Mythic Wildlife does not cover wildlife from the Halo 
franchise that are weak or can’t fight back. With one obvious exception when you look through the book.  Hopefully this will also give inspiration to 
GMs to expand wildlife encounters. Another option to help expand Mythic Wildlife is the Atomic Age bestiary, which sports a massive 35 page bestiary  
of just creatures, alone.
